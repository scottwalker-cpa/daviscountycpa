About The Academy

Fostering Transparency, Trust, and Mutual Respect

The Davis County Citizens Police Academy (DCCPA) offers residents a comprehensive, hands-on understanding of how local law enforcement, criminal justice, and emergency medical services operate across our communities.

In-Depth Education

Learn directly from active-duty officers, detectives, prosecutors, and command staff who serve Davis County every single day.

Interactive Dialogue

Ask questions, explore police protocols, and understand the legal and safety standards guiding split-second decision-making.

Hands-On Field Work

Experience EVOC driving simulators, firearms safety exercises, crime scene investigation techniques, and live K9 search demonstrations.

Program Breakdown

15-Week Interactive Training Areas

Explore the key modules covered during the 15-week academy course.

Duration 15 Consecutive Weeks
Weekly Commitment 1 Night / Wk (3 Hours)
Course Registration Fee $25 (Includes T-Shirt)
Class Schedule Note Weekly topics subject to department & instructor availability

Patrol & Field Operations

Gain firsthand knowledge of day-to-day patrol duties, traffic stops, and officer safety protocols.

  • Simulated traffic stop procedures
  • DUI detection & field sobriety testing
  • Ride-along orientation & safety training

Specialized Units & Demos

Observe specialized unit handlers and tactical equipment operating in high-demand environments.

  • Police K9 tracking & narcotics detection
  • Search & Rescue (SAR) capabilities
  • Bomb squad & tactical equipment displays

Tactical & EVOC Simulator

Hands-on practical training covering crisis response, driving dynamics, and decision-making under stress.

  • SWAT tactics & Crisis Negotiation overview
  • EVOC emergency driving simulator
  • Shoot / Don't Shoot interactive range exercise

Investigations & Forensics

Step into the shoes of crime scene investigators and detectives solving complex criminal cases.

  • Latent fingerprint dusting & lifting
  • Crime scene processing & evidence logging
  • Narcotics & Special Victims Unit (SVU) briefs

Judicial System & Corrections

Understand constitutional law, prosecution processes, and jail operations across Davis County.

  • Davis County Attorney's Office presentation
  • Constitutional law & search warrants
  • Davis County Jail facility walk-through

Dispatch & Fire / EMS Integration

Discover how 911 communications centers coordinate rapid multi-agency emergency responses.

  • 911 dispatch center tour & call routing
  • South Davis Metro Fire / EMS operations
  • County-wide disaster & crisis management

Unified Public Safety

Participating Agencies & Coalition

The DCCPA is proudly hosted in partnership across Davis County municipal police departments, county public safety divisions, and emergency services.

Academy Eligibility Standards

To maintain safety and operational security across facilities and ride-alongs, all prospective students must satisfy the following admission requirements prior to acceptance.

Age Requirement (18+) Applicants must be 18 years or older (17-year-olds are eligible if accompanied by a parent/guardian).
Residency or Employment Must reside, work, or attend school in Davis County, Utah.
Background Clearance Must pass a criminal background check (no felony convictions or disqualifying criminal history).
